OCTOBER 18 · A LETTER FOR YOU

To my once-favorite person,

Happy birthday, Stranger.

I hope life has been gentle with you. I hope you find reasons to smile, people who understand you, and little moments that remind you how beautiful life can still be.

It's strange how someone can go from being a part of your everyday life to becoming someone you quietly wish well from a distance. We may not be what we used to be, but that doesn't mean the memories suddenly mean nothing.

Thank you for the moments that made me happy, for the memories I will carry softly, and even for the lessons I had to learn after everything changed. A part of me will always be grateful that our paths crossed.

There are things I still can't put into perfect words, and maybe I don't need to anymore. Today isn't about asking for anything back. It's simply about wishing you happiness, peace, and all the good things you deserve.

I hope this new chapter brings you closer to the person you want to become. Please take care of yourself, even on the days when life feels heavy. I truly hope you're happy, wherever life takes you.

Once, you were someone I called home. Now, I am learning to keep the good memories without asking them to become the present again.

So, happy birthday. No expectations, no promises—just a sincere wish that life treats you kindly.

With a quiet kind of care,
someone who once knew you by heart. ♡
